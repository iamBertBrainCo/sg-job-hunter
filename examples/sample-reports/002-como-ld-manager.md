## Job Description (archived verbatim)
The Learning & Development Manager will design, develop, and deliver comprehensive training programmes to enhance service excellence, build internal training capability, and drive continuous improvement across all GrandStay properties in Singapore.

Key Responsibilities:
1. Training Programme Design & Delivery: Develop and deliver blended learning programmes covering service culture, guest engagement, product knowledge, safety and hygiene, and operational SOPs.
2. SOP Development: Create brand-aligned SOPs and training materials that ensure consistency across all properties.
3. Internal Capability Building: Coach managers to build internal training capability and sustain learning culture.
4. Training Audits: Conduct training audits and implement mystery audit programmes to measure service standards.
5. Data-Driven Improvements: Present monthly training insights to senior management and use data to drive continuous improvement.

Requirements:
- Minimum 5 years L&D experience in hospitality or related industry
- Proven track record in blended learning design and delivery
- Strong facilitation and communication skills
- Experience with training audits and service excellence programmes
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Education, or related field
