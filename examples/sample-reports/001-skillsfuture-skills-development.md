## Job Description (archived verbatim)
Manager plays a pivotal role in fostering a vibrant skills development ecosystem, aimed at supporting enterprises in their upskilling endeavours as an integral part of their business strategy. This position involves enhancing awareness and adoption of skills development messages and initiatives — particularly among small and medium enterprises (SMEs).

Responsibilities:
1. Strategy Development and Implementation: Formulate and implement actionable plans to cultivate a dynamic skills development ecosystem that aids enterprises in their upskilling initiatives. Engage with enterprises and intermediaries to grasp on-the-ground challenges concerning jobs and skills.
2. Solutioning: Contribute to development and implementation of schemes to support enterprises in the upskilling of their employees. Aid in the development of intermediaries' capability and expertise to encourage and facilitate skills development adoption by enterprises.
3. Enterprise Engagement: Account-manage intermediaries (e.g. larger enterprises) to boost skills development awareness and adoption among SMEs. Promote skills development through participation in events, forums, and other engagement platforms.

Competencies: Strategic Thinking and Planning, Problem Solving and Innovation, Project Management, Stakeholder Engagement and Management.
