## Key Principles

### 1. Quantify Everything
- ❌ "Responsible for training programmes"
- ✅ "Designed and delivered training programmes for 500+ employees with 4.8/5 satisfaction score"

### 2. Use Action Verbs
- Designed, Built, Led, Authored, Created, Transformed
- Not: "Responsible for", "Helped with", "Assisted in"

### 3. Lead with Impact
- What you achieved, not just what you did
- Numbers, percentages, scales, outcomes

### 4. Tailor to the Role
- Highlight relevant experience for each application
- Match your skills to the job description
- Tell your story in a way that resonates
