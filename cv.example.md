## Resume Writing Tips for Singapore Market

### Do:
- ✅ Quantify everything (e.g., "~1,800 jobseekers" not "many people")
- ✅ Use action verbs (Designed, Built, Led, Authored)
- ✅ Lead with impact, not duties
- ✅ Include relevant certifications (e.g., project management, training credentials)
- ✅ Mention Singapore-specific experience (e.g., trade unions, government agencies)

### Don't:
- ❌ List duties without impact
- ❌ Use vague language ("responsible for", "helped with")
- ❌ Include outdated or irrelevant roles
- ❌ Lie or exaggerate (background checks are common)
- ❌ Make it longer than 2 pages

### Singapore-Specific:
- Mention CPF, EP, S Pass if relevant
- Include local certifications (WSQ, etc.)
- Highlight experience with local companies/agencies
- Show understanding of Singapore business culture
