# 5-Day Job Search Mastery Programme (AI Agent-First)

## For Singapore Professionals

**Designed by:** Bertram Yang — Project Creator  
**Connect:** [LinkedIn](https://linkedin.com/in/bertramyang)
**Target:** All PMET professionals in Singapore and ASEAN
**Outcome:** 4+ tailored applications ready to submit by Day 5

---

## Programme Philosophy

> "The system works much better when it knows you well. Think of it as onboarding a recruiter — the first week I need to learn about you, then I become invaluable."

This programme teaches job seekers to:
1. **Think systematically** about their career targets
2. **Use AI agents** to discover and evaluate opportunities
3. **Create quality applications** that stand out
4. **Track their pipeline** professionally
5. **Learn from patterns** to improve over time

**No CLI required.** Everything runs through conversation with the AI agent.

## Day 1: Profile Setup & CV Optimization

**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Outcome:** Personalized profile + optimized CV

### Morning Session (1.5 hours)

#### 1.1 Introduction (15 min)
- Why systematic job search beats random applying
- Quality over quantity: 4 great applications > 40 generic ones
- Overview of the 6-block evaluation framework

#### 1.2 Setting Up Your Profile (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "I want to find L&D jobs in Singapore"
- AI agent installs everything automatically (2-3 minutes)
- AI agent asks: "Tell me about your experience"
- AI agent creates `cv.md`, `config/profile.yml`, `modes/_profile.md`
- Review your profile with the AI agent

#### 1.3 CV Optimization (45 min)
- AI agent shows your CV preview
- Review and edit your CV through conversation
- Key principles:
  - Lead with impact, not duties
  - Quantify everything (e.g., "~1,800 jobseekers" not "many people")
  - Use action verbs (Designed, Built, Led, Authored)
  - Remove outdated or irrelevant roles

### Afternoon Session (1 hour)

#### 1.4 Target Role Definition (30 min)
- Tell the AI agent your primary targets (3-5 roles you'd take tomorrow)
- Tell the AI agent your secondary targets (5-10 roles you'd consider)
- Tell the AI agent roles to avoid (sales, security, engineering if not your field)

#### 1.5 Company Research (30 min)
- AI agent shows 163+ pre-configured companies
- Add your target companies through conversation
- Research 10 companies you want to work for

### Homework
- [ ] Complete your CV with quantified achievements
- [ ] List 20 target companies
- [ ] Update your LinkedIn profile to match your CV

## Day 2: Job Discovery & Screening

**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Outcome:** 20+ matched jobs from automated scan

### Morning Session (1.5 hours)

#### 2.1 Running the Scanner (30 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Find me some jobs"
- AI agent runs scanner automatically
- AI agent presents results: "Found 28 jobs matching your profile"
- Review top matches with the AI agent

#### 2.2 Manual Discovery (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Search MyCareersFuture for L&D jobs"
- AI agent searches and presents results
- Add interesting jobs to your pipeline through conversation

#### 2.3 LinkedIn Discovery (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Search LinkedIn for L&D jobs in Singapore"
- AI agent searches and presents results
- Save 10+ interesting jobs to your pipeline

### Afternoon Session (1 hour)

#### 2.4 Initial Screening (30 min)
- AI agent filters jobs based on your targets
- Remove obvious mismatches (wrong industry, wrong level)
- Keep 20-30 jobs for detailed evaluation

#### 2.5 Company Research (30 min)
- For each remaining job, AI agent researches the company
- What do they do?
- What's their culture like?
- Are they growing or shrinking?
- Do they have LinkedIn employees you know?

### Homework
- [ ] Run the scanner and save results
- [ ] Add 20+ jobs to your pipeline
- [ ] Research top 10 companies

## Day 3: Evaluation & Decision Making

**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Outcome:** Scored shortlist of 5-8 roles

### Morning Session (1.5 hours)

#### 3.1 The 6-Block Framework (30 min)
- Deep dive into each block:
  - **A: Role Match** — Does this title match your targets?
  - **B: Experience Match** — Do you have the required years/skills?
  - **C: Skills Match** — Specific technical/functional skills
  - **D: Location & Logistics** — Can you actually work there?
  - **E: Compensation** — Is the salary in your range?
  - **F: Culture & Mission Fit** — Do you want to work here?

#### 3.2 Evaluating Your First Job (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Evaluate this job"
- AI agent scores the job 1-5 and generates a report
- Review the report with the AI agent
- Understand why each block got its score

#### 3.3 Evaluating More Jobs (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Evaluate the top 5 jobs"
- AI agent evaluates 5-10 more jobs
- Look for patterns:
  - What blocks do you score high on?
  - What blocks do you score low on?
  - Are there skills you keep missing?

### Afternoon Session (1 hour)

#### 3.4 Pattern Analysis (30 min)
- AI agent analyzes your evaluation patterns
- Review your strengths and gaps
- Adjust your targeting if needed

#### 3.5 Final Shortlist (30 min)
- Select your top 4-8 roles (score 4.0+)
- For each role, write why it's a good fit
- Plan your application strategy

### Homework
- [ ] Evaluate 10+ jobs
- [ ] Create your final shortlist of 4-8 roles
- [ ] Identify any skill gaps to address

## Day 4: Application Package Creation

**Duration:** 2-3 hours
**Outcome:** Tailored CVs + cover letters for top 4 roles

### Morning Session (1.5 hours)

#### 4.1 Tailored CV Creation (45 min)
- Tell the AI agent: "Generate applications for the top 4 jobs"
- AI agent generates tailored CVs for each role
- Review each CV with the AI agent
- Key principles:
  - Match the job description's language
  - Emphasize relevant experience
  - Remove or de-emphasize irrelevant roles

#### 4.2 Cover Letter Creation (45 min)
- AI agent generates cover letters for each role
- Review each cover letter with the AI agent
- Key principles:
  - Be specific, not generic
  - Use their language, not yours
  - Show, don't tell

#### 4.3 Application Email Creation (30 min)
- AI agent drafts application emails for each role
- Review each email with the AI agent
- Include:
  - Clear subject line
  - Brief introduction
  - Key qualifications (3-4 bullets)
  - Call to action

### Afternoon Session (1 hour)

#### 4.4 PDF Generation (30 min)
- AI agent converts all CVs and cover letters to PDF
- Review each PDF with the AI agent
- Test opening on different devices

#### 4.5 Final Review (30 min)
- Review all 4 application packages with the AI agent
- Check for:
  - Consistency across documents
  - Accuracy of claims
  - Professional formatting
  - No typos or errors

### Homework
- [ ] Generate tailored CVs for top 4 roles
- [ ] Write cover letters for top 4 roles
- [ ] Create application emails for top 4 roles
- [ ] Convert all to PDF

## Teaching Notes

### For Career Facilitators

This programme is designed to be taught in a group setting (8-12 learners) or 1-on-1.

#### Group Teaching Tips
- Start with your own job search as an example
- Have learners work in pairs on evaluation exercises
- Do live demonstrations of the AI agent workflow
- Encourage learners to share their target companies

#### 1-on-1 Coaching Tips
- Spend more time on Day 1 (profile setup)
- Help learners identify their "superpower"
- Review each evaluation report together
- Role-play interview questions based on their profile

#### Common Challenges
- **Learners don't have quantified achievements:** Help them estimate (e.g., "~1,800 jobseekers" is better than "many people")
- **Learners are too broad in targeting:** Help them narrow to 3-5 specific roles
- **Learners want to apply to everything:** Teach the 4.0/5 threshold
- **Learners are afraid of rejection:** Frame rejection as data, not failure

## Appendix: Singapore Job Market Guide

1. **MyCareersFuture** — Government portal, PMET roles, essential
2. **JobStreet Singapore** — Largest private portal
3. **LinkedIn Jobs** — Professional network, 90% of recruiters use this
4. **JobsDB** — Strong in professional & management roles
5. **Glassdoor** — Company reviews + jobs
6. **Glints** — Tech, startup, and modern professional roles
7. **CareerPage** — Local corporate & government roles
8. **JobCentral** — 1M monthly page views
9. **Indeed** — Aggregator, wide coverage
